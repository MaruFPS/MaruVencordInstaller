Friends Sidebar Setup
  1. Enable Vencord’s Experiments plugin.
  2. Open Discord’s experimental settings, find 2026-09-friends-sidebar, and set Variant Override → Variant 1.
  3. Enable Vencord’s PinDMs and MaruFriendsSidebar plugins.
  4. Restart Discord when required, then create/select your PinDM categories in the Friends sidebar.

MaruFriendsSidebar does not automatically enable PinDMs. These settings remain under your control.

A warning that the server did not supply an experiment configuration does not automatically mean your manual override failed. Verify that the Friends sidebar appears.

This setup reflects the working October 10, 2026 configuration. Discord may change or remove its experimental interface in future updates. This guide remains available after installation.

YouTube Music Setup
✓ YouTube Music Controls included
In YTMDesktop, make sure all four of these options are enabled:
• Companion Server
• Allow Browser Communication
• Enable Companion Auth
• Discord Rich Presence
After enabling them, open Discord and approve the YTMDesktop authorization prompt. Then fully restart Discord. The YouTube Music player and activity should appear automatically.
